# Introduction to Electrodynamics / 기초전자기학 (Griffiths 1989)

> 원저: **David J. Griffiths** (University of California, Davis)
> 원서: Prentice Hall, 1989 (2nd ed.)
> 공역: 임채호·김진승·이영희 (3인 공역)
> 513p

## 개요
**전자기학(전자정전기+전자기파)** — 물리 관점의 표준 교과서. **벡터 해석(제1장)** → **정전기학(제2장)** → **퍼텐셜 해석 기법(제3장: Laplace eq., image method, separation of variables, multipoles)** → **물질의 전자기장(제4장: polarization, P)** → **정자기학(제5장: Lorentz force, Biot-Savart, ∇×B, Ampere, vector potential, inductance)** → **물질의 자기장(제6장: magnetization, H field)** → **시간진행 전자기(제7장: Faraday, Maxwell-Ampere, EM waves)** → **전자기파(제8장: Poynting, radiation, dipole radiation, Larmor formula, antennas)** → **방사(제9장)** → **전자기학과 상대론(제10장: special relativity, four-vector, EM field tensor)** `[raw/books/basic-em.md]`.

## 장 구조 (TOC 기반, 513p)
1. **Vector Analysis** — ∇ operator, divergence/curl, gradients
2. **Electrostatics** — Coulomb, field lines, Gauss, potential, dielectrics
3. **Techniques for Solving for Potential** — Laplace eq., **image method**, separation of variables, multipoles
4. **Electrostatic Fields in Matter** — polarization, P, boundary
5. **Magnetostatics** — Lorentz force, **Biot-Savart**, ∇×B, Ampere, vector potential, inductance
6. **Magnetism in Matter** — magnetization, H field, magnetic materials
7. **Electrodynamics** — Ohm's law, continuity, **Faraday, Maxwell-Ampere**, EM waves
8. **Electromagnetic Waves** — wave equation, **Poynting**, **dipole radiation**, **Larmor formula**, antennas
9. **Radiation** — dipole radiation, multipoles, scattering
10. **Electrodynamics and Relativity** — special relativity, four-vector, EM field tensor
