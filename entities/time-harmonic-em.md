# Time-Harmonic Electromagnetic Fields (Harrington 1990)

> 저자: **Roger F. Harrington** (Professor of Electrical Engineering, Syracuse University)
> 출판: McGraw-Hill, Inc. — MHEEES series (F. E. Terman, consulting editor), 1990, 495p

## 개요
**시간 조화(time-harmonic) 전자기장**의 수학적 이론을 축으로 삼고, **파 공간(wave space)** — 평면파·원뿔파·구파 — 의 **완전함수(completeness) 전개**로 전자기 문제를 풀 수 있게 하는 고전 이론서. 제1장은 **기초 개념**(basic equations, constitutive relationships, complex quantities/equations/constitutive parameters, complex power, A-C characteristics, singularities)으로 수학적 토대를 닦고, 제2장은 **파(wave)**(wave equation, perfect dielectrics, intrinsic wave constants, lossy media, reflection, transmission-line concepts, waveguide, resonator, radiation, antenna concepts)의 전체 지형을 훑는다 `[raw/books/time-harmonic-em.md]`. 이후 **theorem·개념**(제3장: duality, uniqueness, image theory, equivalence principle, induction theorem) → **평면파 함수(제4장)** → **원뿔파(제5장)** → **구파(제6장)** → **마이크로파 네트워크(제8장)** 로 전개.

## 장 구조 (TOC 기반, 495p)
1. **Fundamental Concepts** — basic equations, constitutive relationships, generalized current, energy/power, circuit concepts, complex quantities, A-C characteristics, singularities
2. **Introduction to Waves** — wave equation, perfect dielectrics, intrinsic wave constants, lossy matter, reflection, waveguide, resonator, radiation, antenna concepts
3. **Some Theorems and Concepts** — source concept, **duality**, uniqueness, **image theory**, equivalence principle, half-space fields, induction theorem
4. **Plane Wave Functions**
5. **Cylindrical Wave Functions**
6. **Spherical Wave Functions**
7. (TOC에서 제7장 표기 없음)
8. **Microwave Networks** — cylindrical waveguides
