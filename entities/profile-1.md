# Profile 1 (Oxford Business English)

> OUP가 낸 **비즈니스 영어** 교재. Pre-Intermediate 수준, 142p. 영문 본문이 고가독성이며 `[[business-english]]`의 6개 유닛을 Talking Business → Vocabulary → Reading → Listening → Grammar 순서로 전개.

## 개요
- **Title:** Profile — Pre-Intermediate Student's Book
- **Authors:** Jon Naunton, Mark Tulip
- **Publisher:** Oxford University Press
- **수준:** Pre-Intermediate
- **쪽수:** 142 (raw 기준 페이지 마커 142개)

## 교육 구조 (유닛별, p2–p3 목차 근거)
각 유닛이 5개 기술 영역을 고정 순서로 훈련시킨다:
| 유닛 | TALKING BUSINESS | GRAMMAR (p2–p3 근거) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — A new job | Worries on your first day in a new job | `be`, `have`, `have got`; present simple (routines) |
| 2 — Organizations | Companies with no hierarchy | Present simple & continuous |
| 3 — Quality & roles | Brands | Countable/uncountable nouns |
| 4 — Money | Personal finance questionnaire | (countable/uncountable, banking vocab) |
| 5 — Import-Export | Importing / Exporting & markets | (market vocab, reading: niche markets) |
| 6 — Travel | Personal banking / Travel | Business travel vs. tourism |

- 각 유닛은 **Reading**(Dream jobs, Typical working day, ISO standards) + **Listening**(Welcoming new recruits, Bank transactions, Upmarket/downmarket)을 병행 배치.
- **학습법:** "Talking Business" 대화 맥락 → 실제 비즈니스 표현(Vocabulary) → Reading/Listening으로 확장 → Grammar(문법 지점)의 순회. 이는 `[[english-grammar-patterns]]`와 `[[english-listening-strategy]]`와 결합되는 실무형 구조.
