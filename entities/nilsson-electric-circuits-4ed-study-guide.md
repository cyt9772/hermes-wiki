# Electric Circuits, 4th ed. — Study Guide (James W. Nilsson)

> James W. Nilsson (Professor Emeritus, Iowa State University), *Electric Circuits* 4th ed.의 **동반 연습문제(Supplementary Problems) 풀이**를 담은 스타디 가이드. 441p. Addison-Wesley. 서문: "This study guide was written to accompany the Electric Circuits, Fourth Edition; its goal is to give you additional insight and experience in solving circuit problems. The study guide contains illustrative drill exercises and problems that are similar to but not identical to the drill exercises and problems in the textbook. A discussion of each solution is given so that you can trace…" [raw/books/nilsson-electric-circuits-4ed-study-guide.md].

## 개요
**텍스트북 20장 + "Solutions to Supplementary Problems"** 구조. 장 구조 (원문 p.5 TOC): Ch.1 Circuit Variables (p.1) · Ch.2 Circuit Elements (p.9) · Ch.3 Simple Resistive Circuits (p.21) · Ch.4 Techniques of Circuit Analysis (p.35) · Ch.5 Topology in Circuit Analysis (p.57) · Ch.6 The Operational Amplifier (p.69) · Ch.7 Inductance and Capacitance (p.87) · Ch.8 Response of First-Order RL and RC Circuits (p.101) · Ch.9 Natural and Step Responses of RLC Circuits (p.133) · Ch.10 Sinusoidal Steady-State Analysis (p.149) · Ch.11 Sinusoidal Steady-State Power Calculations (p.167) · Ch.12 Balanced Three-Phase Circuits (p.181) · Ch.13 Mutual Inductance (p.195) · Ch.14 Series and Parallel Resonance (p.209) · Ch.15 Introduction to the Laplace Transform (p.227) · Ch.16 The Laplace Transform in Circuit Analysis (p.243) · Ch.17 The Transfer Function (p.263) · Ch.18 Fourier Series (p.281) · Ch.19 The Fourier Transform (p.303) · Ch.20 Two-Port Circuits (p.321) + **Solutions to Supplementary Problems (p.341)**.

## 핵심
- "drill exercises and problems **similar to but not identical to** the textbook" (서문) — **원서와 별개의 추가 문제집** + 풀이. 원서 [[nilsson-electric-circuits-4ed-solutions-vol1]](4판 100% 원문)의 **연습문제 풀이**와 역할이 겹치지 않음 (원서 연습문제 풀이 vs. 이 스타디가이드의 추가문제 풀이).
- **DC(1–6장) → AC·동역(7–14장) → Laplace·Fourier·두포트(15–20장)** — 위키의 [[dc-ac-circuit-analysis]]·[[laplace-transform-circuits]]와 일치.
- 20장 "두포트" 종결 + 독립 "Supplementary Solutions" 부록 → **자습·시험 대비용** 구조.
