# Engineering Electromagnetics (Hayt 5th ed.)

> 저자: **William H. Hayt, Jr.** (Purdue University)
> 출판: McGraw-Hill (International Editions), 5th ed., 492p
> note: "The problems in this edition are all new... over 30 years of usage..." (5판)

## 개요
**공학 전자기의 표준 교과서.** 벡터 해석(제1장) → Coulomb 장(제2장) → **Gauss·Divergence**(제3장) → Energy·Potential·Gradient(제4장) → **Conductors·Dielectrics·Capacitance**(제5장) → **Experimental Mapping**(curvilinear squares, conformal mapping, 제6장) → **Poisson·Laplace**(제7장) → **Steady Magnetic Field — Biot-Savart, H·∇×H, B·∇·B, forces, energy, inductance**(제8장) → **Magnetic Forces·Materials·Inductance**(제9장) → **Time-Varying Fields and Maxwell's Equations — Faraday, displacement current, four equations, potentials, plane waves, retarded potentials**(제10장) → **Uniform Plane Wave(제11장)** → **Transmission Lines(제12장)** → **Applications of Maxwell's Equations(제13장)** `[raw/books/em-engineering.md]`.

## 장 구조 (TOC 기반, 492p)
1. **Vector Analysis** — scalars/vectors, Cartesian/cylindrical/spherical, dot/cross product, vector field
2. **Coulomb's Law and Electric Field Intensity** — E intensity, volume/line/sheet charge, streamlines
3. **Electric Flux Density, Gauss's Law, and Divergence** — D, Gauss, divergence, Maxwell first equation
4. **Energy and Potential** — line integral, potential, gradient
5. **Conductors, Dielectrics, and Capacitance** — J, Ohm, continuity, dielectric polarization, P and D, boundary, capacitance, energy
6. **Experimental Mapping Methods** — curvilinear squares, conformal mapping
7. **Poisson's and Laplace's Equations** — uniqueness, numerical solutions
8. **The Steady Magnetic Field** — **Biot-Savart, ∇×H, ∇·B, forces, energy, inductance**
9. **Magnetic Forces, Materials, and Inductance** — Lorentz force, materials, inductance
10. **Time-Varying Fields and Maxwell's Equations** — **Faraday, displacement current, four equations, potentials, plane waves, retarded potentials**
11. **The Uniform Plane Wave** — oblique incidence, boundary conditions, intrinsic impedance
12. **Transmission Lines** — characteristic impedance, reflection/transmission
13. **Several Other Applications of Maxwell's Equations** — waveguides, antennas intro

## OCR 신뢰도
- **주의**: 1999 5판 McGraw-Hill. vector/matrix 수식은 부분 깨짐, Maxwell 4방정식/∇ operator 표기는 원본 대조
