# Microwave Engineering (Pozar 1990)

> 저자: **David M. Pozar** (University of Massachusetts at Amherst)
> 출판: Addison-Wesley Publishing Company (Addison-Wesley Series in E&CE), 1990 1st ed. (1993-09 reprint), 742p, ISBN 0-201-50418-9

## 개요
마이크로파 공학의 **전체 스펙트럼**을 한 권에 담은 표준 교재. **전자기 이론(제2장: Maxwell, fields in media, plane waves, polarization, energy)** → **전송선로 이론(제3장: lumped-element, Smith chart, quarter-wave transformer)** → **선로·도파관(제4장: microstrip, stripline, waveguide modes)** → **마이크로파 네트워크(제5장: S-parameters)** → **임피던스 정합(제6장)** → **파워 디바이더/결합기/하이브리드(제7장: rat-race, Wilkinson, 90/180 hybrids)** → **공진기(제8장: cavity, Q, crystal oscillators)** → **능동 회로(제9장: amplifiers, oscillators, noise figure)** → **필터(제10장: low-pass prototypes, Chebyshev/Butterworth, coupled-line)** → **페라이트(제11장: isolator, circulator)** → **검파기/주파수 변환기(제12장)** 순. 13장 Microwave Systems Intro로 마무리 `[raw/books/microwave-engineering.md]`.

## 장 구조 (TOC 기반, 742p)
1. **Introduction** — what is microwave engineering, applications, history
2. **Electromagnetic Theory** — Maxwell, fields in media, boundary conditions, wave equation, plane waves (lossy/good conductor), polarization, energy
3. **Transmission Line Theory** — lumped-element model, Smith chart, terminated lossless line, quarter-wave transformer
4. **Transmission Lines and Waveguides** — microstrip, stripline, waveguide modes
5. **Microwave Network Analysis** — S-parameters, cascading, scattering
6. **Impedance Matching and Tuning** — stub, quarter-wave, multi-section
7. **Power Dividers, Couplers, and Hybrids** — branch/series line, rat-race, **Wilkinson**, 90/180 hybrids
8. **Microwave Resonators** — cavity resonators, Q, coupling, crystal oscillators
9. **Active Microwave Circuits** — amplifiers, oscillators, noise figure
10. **Filter Theory and Design** — low-pass prototypes, **Chebyshev/Butterworth**, coupled-line
11. **Ferrite Components** — **isolators, circulators**
12. **Detectors and Mixers**
13. **Introduction to Microwave Systems**
